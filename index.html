WHY SWAP SKILLS?

Your knowledge is worth something. Use it.

Paying for every new skill adds up quickly. A skill exchange lets you learn by giving back what you already know.

Paying for lessons

  • Every lesson costs money, week after week
  • You are only ever the student
  • Fixed courses that may not fit what you need

Swapping skills

  • Costs your time, not your money
  • Teaching builds your confidence and deepens what you know
  • Learn practical, real-world skills from real people
  • Meet people outside your usual circle

        TRUST & SAFETY

        Built on trust between people who are just getting to know each other.

        Lessons happen between members of the community, so trust is part of the design from day one. This is how the pilot would keep learners and teachers safe. In this demo, verification, reviews and reports are simulated and stay on your device.

        ✓

        Verified profiles

        Every member verifies a university email address before they can teach or book.

        ★

        Ratings & reviews

        Only learners who completed a lesson can rate it, so feedback comes from real experience.

        ⚑

        Report a problem

        Report inappropriate behaviour or a safety concern from any lesson, at any time.

        ↺

        Token-back promise

        If a lesson doesn't match its description, report it and your token is returned.

        Community guidelines

        1. Be respectful. Treat every learner and teacher with patience and kindness. No harassment, discrimination or pressure of any kind.
        2. Describe lessons honestly. Only offer skills you are genuinely comfortable teaching, and make sure the listing matches what you deliver.
        3. Meet safely. Start online or meet in a public place such as a library or campus café. Never share your home address before you feel comfortable.
        4. Keep it token-only. No cash, gifts or side payments. Every lesson costs exactly one token.
        5. Show up or cancel early. Cancel at least 24 hours ahead so the other person isn't left waiting.
        6. Speak up. If something feels wrong, end the session and report it. Reports are reviewed by the team.

        What makes a good lesson?

        • 🎯
          One clear outcome

          "By the end, you'll be able to…" Keep it to one achievable goal.

        • ⏱
          The right length

          Fit the topic to 15, 30 or 60 minutes. Less is more for a first lesson.

        • 🧭
          Start where they are

          Ask what they already know in the first two minutes and adjust.

        • 🛠
          Hands-on practice

          Let the learner try it themselves rather than just watching you.

        • 📝
          Something to take away

          A cheat sheet, recipe or next step helps the lesson stick.

        GOOD TO KNOW

        Frequently asked questions.

        Do I need to be an expert to teach?

        No. In the proposed marketplace, you would share a skill you are comfortable explaining to a beginner. Describe your experience honestly.

        How much does a lesson cost?

        The demo uses one token per lesson, regardless of the displayed duration. Tokens are not money and have no cash value. We are testing whether this exchange feels fair.

        Can I book a real lesson today?

        Not yet. The current website is a university pretotype with sample listings and simulated tokens. Pilot interest is separate from a demo booking.

        How do you keep members safe?

        In the proposed pilot, every member verifies a university email, only learners who completed a lesson can review it, and anyone can report inappropriate behaviour or a lesson that doesn't match its description. See the Trust & safety section and community guidelines.

        Where are my tokens and responses stored?

        Demo activity and any locally saved pilot response are stored on this browser only. No account, central database or payment system is connected.
