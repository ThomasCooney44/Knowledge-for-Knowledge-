How it works

Share what you know. Learn what you love.

Every lesson costs one token, and the only way to earn tokens is to teach. Nobody pays, and everybody gives something back.

  1. 1
    Teach a skill

    List a short lesson in something you know well, from Spanish to Excel to photography.

  2. 2
    Earn a token

    Complete a teaching session to earn one token. In this demo you can simulate that.

  3. 3
    Learn from someone else

    Spend your token on another student's lesson and keep the exchange moving.

This is a demo. Tokens, offers and bookings are simulated and stay in this browser. No real lessons, accounts or payments are created.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to be an expert to teach?

No. In the proposed marketplace, you would share a skill you are comfortable explaining to a beginner. Describe your experience honestly.

How much does a lesson cost?

The demo uses one token per lesson, regardless of the displayed duration. Tokens are not money and have no cash value. We are testing whether this exchange feels fair.

Can I book a real lesson today?

Not yet. The current website is a university pretotype with sample listings and simulated tokens. Pilot interest is separate from a demo booking.

Where are my tokens and responses stored?

Demo activity and any locally saved pilot response are stored on this browser only. No account, central database or payment system is connected.

About this project. Knowledge for Knowledge is a university project pretotype. No real bookings, payments or token transfers take place.
