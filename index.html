WHY SWAP SKILLS?

Your knowledge is worth something. Use it.

Paying for every new skill adds up quickly. A skill exchange lets you learn by giving back what you already know.

Paying for lessons

  • Every lesson costs money, week after week
  • You are only ever the student
  • Fixed courses that may not fit what you need

Swapping skills

  • Costs your time, not your money
  • Teaching builds your confidence and deepens what you know
  • Learn practical, real-world skills from real people
  • Meet people outside your usual circle

      GOOD TO KNOW

      Frequently asked questions.

      Do I need to be an expert to teach?

      No. In the proposed marketplace, you would share a skill you are comfortable explaining to a beginner. Describe your experience honestly.

      How much does a lesson cost?

      The demo uses one token per lesson, regardless of the displayed duration. Tokens are not money and have no cash value. We are testing whether this exchange feels fair.

      Can I book a real lesson today?

      Not yet. The current website is a university pretotype with sample listings and simulated tokens. Pilot interest is separate from a demo booking.

      Where are my tokens and responses stored?

      Demo activity and any locally saved pilot response are stored on this browser only. No account, central database or payment system is connected.
